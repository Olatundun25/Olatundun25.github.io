Customer Churn & Regression Dashboard

Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze customer churn, revenue loss, and factors associated with customer attrition. The dashboard examines churn across contract type, internet service, payment method, tenure, monthly charges, and customer demographics.

Key Insights

  • Analyzed 7,043 total customers and 1,869 churned customers.
  • Identified an overall customer churn rate of 26.54%.
  • Analyzed approximately 139.13K in revenue lost from customer churn.
  • Compared churn patterns across contract types, internet services, payment methods, tenure groups, and monthly charges.
